Symbolic-Guided Disentanglement for One-Shot Timbre Transfer and Controllable Music Generation

Supplementary webpage with audio examples.

Anonymous authors (paper currently under review)

Abstract

Controllable music generation often relies on learning disentangled representations of musical structure and timbre, enabling one-shot timbre transfer and interpretable control. Existing methods typically learn these representations in unsupervised or semi-supervised settings, relying on architectural biases, data augmentations, staged training, and adversarial objectives. However, significant information leakage between representations remains, limiting their suitability for reliable timbre transfer. We introduce a symbolic-guided framework that explicitly incorporates MIDI information to guide the disentanglement process. Our method combines structure-preserving augmentations, pitch supervision, and a contrastive timbre objective within the latent space of a pretrained music autoencoder, with output latents generated by a diffusion transformer trained under a rectified-flow objective. We evaluate representation properties alongside reconstruction and timbre transfer, including experiments on real-world audio with automatically estimated transcriptions. Results show that symbolic guidance produces more informative and better disentangled representations while improving one-shot timbre transfer and maintaining high reconstruction quality.
